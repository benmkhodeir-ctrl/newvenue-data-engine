# Enrichment audit plan

## Objective

Determine what level of commercially useful enrichment can be produced reliably for 30–50 genuine upcoming NSW hospitality venues before deciding what customer-facing product or price exists.

## Required fields to test

- venue/proposed venue name
- address
- venue type
- opening signal and date
- likely opening stage
- operating company / ABN / ACN
- owner/operator where reliably supported
- related or existing venues
- website
- social profile
- public business phone
- public business email
- other business contact information where legitimately public/obtainable
- confidence level
- source/provenance

## Confidence vocabulary

- `confirmed`: directly supported by an authoritative or first-party source.
- `strong`: multiple independent signals support the match.
- `probable`: plausible but not strong enough to assert as fact without qualification.
- `unknown`: no reliable support. Leave the value blank.

## Measures

For every field calculate:

- coverage percentage
- confirmed/strong/probable mix
- false-match rate discovered during manual review
- time or cost required to obtain

Across the dataset calculate:

- percentage of raw applications that become genuine new-venue candidates
- percentage with matched DA/planning evidence
- days between first signal and later observed opening where known
- percentage of records that can be processed without human intervention
- most common reasons a record is rejected

## Exit criteria for V0

V0 is successful if we can produce a repeatable 30–50 venue dataset with explicit provenance and enough enrichment coverage to formulate a fixed weekly report. Pricing and customer acquisition testing begin only after this audit.
