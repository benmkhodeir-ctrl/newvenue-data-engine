## What the live trial already shows

1. A new liquor licence is a valuable trigger but is not a reliable proxy for a new venue.
2. Existing venues can lodge new-licence applications, producing false positives for net-new account acquisition.
3. A venue trading today can still be a successful historical lead if the signal preceded opening.
4. Planning/DA evidence materially improves confidence when it corroborates a new licence, fit-out, change-of-use or recently leased hospitality tenancy.
5. Tenancy-level matching matters. A building-level address can contain multiple existing and new businesses simultaneously.
6. Some liquor signals belong to non-restaurant contexts such as entertainment venues or premium coworking facilities and need target filtering.
7. Free enrichment can sometimes produce a commercially strong lead: venue identity, operator/group, named person, expected opening date and public contact surface without a paid enrichment provider.
